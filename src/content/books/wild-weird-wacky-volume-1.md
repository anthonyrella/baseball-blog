Researching the history of baseball will uncover a treasure trove of unusual stories and events. In the *Wild, Weird, Wacky, and Unusual* we explore some of baseball's truly amazing feats and zany events. Stories that will make you laugh, cry, and often shake your head in disbelief. Some of these tales are so incredible, so hilarious, and so unexpected that they deserve to be shared.

Everyone knows about the home run records, the legendary pitchers, and the great championship teams. But fewer people know about the team that boasted four twenty-game winners in a single season, or the pitcher who was struck by lightning, recovered, and finished the game. There's Eddie Collins, who stole six bases in a game—twice!—and Rube Waddell, who once abandoned the pitcher's mound mid-game to chase after a fire truck.

Who could imagine that one of the greatest pitchers in baseball history was once traded for the cost of repairing a fence? Or that another pitcher actually won a batting title? Then there's the tale of the team owner who kidnapped an entire team—and the heartwarming, feel-good story of the Portland Mavericks. Ever wonder how the seventh-inning stretch began, or when "Take Me Out to the Ball Game" became a ballpark staple? Well, these and other fascinating stories can be found in these pages.
